About

I'm a Chartered Professional Accountant (CPA) and Certified General Accountant (CGA), qualified in Canada, with internationally recognized credentials and over 15 years of experience in financial accounting and strategic leadership. I also hold the ACCA (UK), and completed my MBA in England and M.Com & B.Com in India.

As Founder & CEO of Santo Catholic Mission, I've built a global Catholic media apostolate carrying prayer and inspiration to the world in more than ten languages. Yet the truest part of who I am is my faith: nourished daily by the Holy Eucharist, the Holy Rosary, and Eucharistic Adoration. I begin each day with the Rosary in hand, and I share my story simply as a witness — in the hope that one life given to God might encourage another.

My faith & dedication
When I was seven years old, I knelt before Our Lady with my grandmother's rosary and made a promise to the Infant Jesus and the Holy Mother: that if they guided me in life, I would tell the whole world about them. That promise has shaped every day since — my work, my mission, and my every breath are dedicated to keeping it.
Ad Jesum per Mariam — To Jesus, through Mary The promise of a seven-year-old, kept through a lifetime — sustained each day by three devotions.

The Holy Eucharist

The source and summit of my life — I strive to receive Our Lord in Holy Communion as often as each day allows.

The Holy Rosary

Carried with me always. I begin every day in the hands of the Blessed Mother, one decade at a time.

Eucharistic Adoration

Quiet hours before the Blessed Sacrament, where the heart is stilled, renewed, and made ready for the day.
